\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \defname{}, a co-evolving red/blue security gym for multi-agent LLM systems. A
deterministic scoring path isolates measurement from semantic manipulation. Topology governs
spread under forced coordination and propagation. On real backbones, a prompt-level defense
gave action-channel compliance of $0/1075$ decisions but left $\ASRs{=}1.000$ on tree and
star, showing availability sabotage is topology-determined. \defname{} is a replicable
instrument for system-level evaluation of multi-agent collectives.
