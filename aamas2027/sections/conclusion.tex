\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \defname{}, a co-evolving red/blue security gym for multi-agent LLM systems. A
deterministic scoring path isolates measurement from semantic manipulation, and topology
governs spread under forced coordination and propagation. On real backbones a prompt-level
defense gave action-channel compliance of $0/1075$ decisions but left $\ASRs{=}1.000$ on tree
and star, because availability sabotage is determined by topology and by a predicate the model
never influences; with no no-defense arm in the same protocol, that study identifies the
phenomenon rather than the defense's efficacy. \defname{} is a replicable instrument for
system-level evaluation of multi-agent collectives.
