\section{Discussion, Limitations, and Ethics}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{Integrity:} deterministic scoring prevents manipulation but requires well-specified
predicates (\Cref{rem:metric}). \textbf{Validity:} main gap is emulation fidelity
($\kappaem\approx0.48$); the emulated-versus-real-tools ablation is unmeasured; the
deterministic-versus-judge ablation is reported. \textbf{Ethics:} only sandboxed fictitious
payloads with bounded adversaries (\Cref{sec:threat}) are used.
