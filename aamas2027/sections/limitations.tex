\section{Limitations and Validity}
\label{sec:limitations}

The headline degradation result (\Cref{tab:degradation}) is measured on a transparent
\emph{synthetic} emulator; real-backbone measurements are PILOT-labelled throughout.
LLM-emulation validity is bounded by human agreement
($\kappaem\approx0.48$); we mitigate by keeping scoring deterministic and running an
emulated-versus-real ablation. Estimators are unbiased, but the product
$\widehat{\NRPs}$ is a nonlinear statistic, so CIs are computed on the log/arcsine
scale and reported alongside point estimates. The topology sweep
(\Cref{tab:topology}) uses star, chain, tree (and mesh in the pilot); more topologies
and $N\in\{20,50\}$ are future work. Batch and configuration-parameter interactions are
not exhaustively swept (exponential complexity; \Cref{app:complexity}). Where a reader
prefers a different aggregator (\Cref{rem:metric}), we report alternatives. Claims are
bounded to the stated configurations and $N$; no superiority claim beyond them is made.
