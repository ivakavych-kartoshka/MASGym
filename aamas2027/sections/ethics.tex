\section{Ethical and Safety Considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

\defname{} is a \emph{measurement} instrument. The red-team primitives it instantiates
(prompt injection, memory poisoning, Sybils, collusion) are drawn from the published
literature and are exactly the skills needed to audit agent systems; they are exercised
only inside the controlled harness against scripted proxies and registered predicates,
never against live third-party services. \defname{} distributes task-input sets that are
benign and synthetic, and it does not generate or disseminate harmful content outside
the sandbox. Our positions are explicit in the threat model (\Cref{sec:threat}): the
adversary is bounded by the seven knobs and episodic budgets; the TCB is the harness.
\defname{}'s ``classes and harm'' channel records social-utility measurements as
first-class evidence---an accepted form of measurement, not an instruction to generate
harm. We advise that results from \defname{} be used to route defenses, not to certify
systems, and that no metric in \defname{} be used for security certification claims
without an independent adversarial review.
