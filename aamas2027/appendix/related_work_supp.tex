\section{Related Work in Full}
\label{app:related-full}

This appendix expands \Cref{sec:related}: it records the multi-agent defenses and the
classical distributed-systems line that motivates our knobs, and it credits the environment
vocabulary this instrument adopts. The positioning against ASB and AgentDojo is in
\Cref{sec:related} and is not repeated.

\subsection{Multi-agent defenses and Byzantine antecedents}
Beyond the per-agent guardrails lifted in the main paper, multi-agent defenses include
AutoDefense~\cite{zeng2024autodefense} and the topology-guided
G-Safeguard~\cite{yu2025gsafeguard}. Classical Byzantine fault
tolerance~\cite{lamport1982byzantine,pease1980reaching} and Byzantine-robust
aggregation~\cite{blanchard2017krum} tolerate faulty fractions under a trusted coordinator
over \emph{numeric} messages; they motivate our adversary-fraction and orchestrator-trust
knobs, but they do not cover collectives that exchange natural language, where the same
fraction of compromised agents can be far more or far less damaging depending on which roles
and which edges they hold. That gap is one of the things the sweeps of
\Cref{sec:experiments} make measurable. Mixing and debating
designs~\cite{du2023debate} are a further collective pattern that \defname{} could host as a
topology variant rather than as a separate environment.

\subsection{Environment vocabulary adopted}
Three design choices are inherited rather than invented, and are credited here so that the
main paper's compression does not read as novelty. From standardized environment
work~\cite{brockman2016openaigym,terry2021pettingzoo,leibo2021meltingpot} \defname{} takes the
step/observation/action interface, the notion of a partially observable multi-agent
environment, and the idea that the \emph{collective} is the unit at which social outcomes are
scored. From automated red teaming~\cite{perez2022redteaming} and the MAST
taxonomy~\cite{cemri2025mast} it takes the vocabulary for group-level failure and the practice
of logging an attack trajectory. From AgentDojo~\cite{debenedetti2024agentdojo} it takes
deterministic, state-based scoring: rather than asking a model whether a system was
compromised, the environment registers a program that reads recorded state. That property is
lifted here from one agent's state to the global trace (\Cref{def:projection},
\Cref{thm:integrity}), which is what makes it usable at the level of a collective.

The parts that are ours, and that no prior instrument provides, are the system-level
aggregation and its axiomatic characterization (\Cref{thm:metric}), the trace projection and
its integrity consequence, the parameterized adversary vector with collusion and
orchestrator-trust knobs, and the co-evolving red/blue protocol of \Cref{sec:algorithm}.
