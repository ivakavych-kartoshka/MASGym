\subsection{Threats to validity in full}
\label{app:limitations}
The main text flags the largest unmeasured gap; this subsection records the rest so the
main paper stays within its page limit.

\emph{Synthetic versus real.} The parameter sweeps in \Cref{app:degradation} are measured
on a transparent \emph{synthetic} emulator, and every table is labelled accordingly. The
defense study of \Cref{sec:defense} is the exception and is measured on real backbones.
Because the deterministic checker, not the emulator, produces the verdict, emulator noise
degrades realism rather than integrity---but realism is still the binding constraint, and
$\kappaem\approx0.48$ is inherited from ToolEmu~\cite{ruan2024toolemu} rather than
re-measured here. The harness registers an emulated-versus-real-tools ablation for
independent re-testing; we did not run it, and we prefer to say so than to let a
registration imply coverage.

\emph{Statistical resolution.} The defense cells use $M{=}30$ episodes, so a reported
$0.00$ is $0/30$, i.e.\ a $95\%$ interval of $[0,0.10]$. The compliance counts that carry
the headline claim are decision-level ($1075$ per backbone) and therefore far tighter:
$0/1075$ gives a Wilson upper bound of $0.0036$. $\widehat{\NRPs}$ is nonlinear in
$\ASRs$, so its intervals are arcsin-scaled beside the point estimates.

\emph{Sample size and degeneracy.} Four backbones is a small sample, and Llama-3.2-1B may
be unrepresentative of small models generally. Three backbones return identical system
values once compliance is zero; we spell this out as a structural degeneracy of the
deterministic availability path rather than present it as independent corroboration.

\emph{Scope of the defense claim.} The defense study measures whether a defense is
\emph{followed}, not whether a system is secure. More topologies, $N\in\{20,50\}$, and
exhaustive knob interactions remain future work; the reachable region is explored rather
than the full exponential knob space (\Cref{cor:budget}).

\subsection{Ethics and safety in full}
\label{app:ethics}
\defname{} is a measurement instrument. Its red-team primitives are drawn from the
published literature and are exercised only inside the sandbox against registered
predicates, never against live third-party services; the payloads are fictitious, so any
successful exfiltration is ours by construction. Tasks are benign, the adversary is
bounded (\Cref{sec:threat}), and released artifacts contain only synthetic identifiers.
Results should route defenses, not certify systems: the metrics in this paper are
measurements of a fixed configuration grid under a stated threat model.

\subsection{Desiderata for the aggregator (D1--D5)}
A system-level utility--security aggregator $g:[0,1]^2\to[0,1]$, written
$\NRPs=g(\PNAs,\,1-\ASRs)$, should satisfy: (D1) \emph{boundedness}, $g$ maps into
$[0,1]$; (D2) \emph{security consistency}, $g(u,1)=u$ and $g(0,\sigma)=0$; (D3)
\emph{monotonicity}, $g$ non-decreasing in each argument; (D4) \emph{utility- and
security-proportionality}, $g(\lambda u,\sigma)=\lambda g(u,\sigma)$ and
$g(u,\lambda\sigma)=\lambda g(u,\sigma)$ for $\lambda\in[0,1]$; and (D5) \emph{reduction},
$g$ recovers \Cref{eq:asb-nrp} at $N=1$.

\subsection{Cost and complexity of the instrument}
\label{app:complexity}
An episode runs $N$ agents for $H$ steps: $\Theta(NH)$ agent invocations and up to
$\Theta(|\Etop|H)$ messages; the trace has size $\Theta((N+|\Etop|)H)$. The checker
evaluates $\Predsec,\Predutil$ once per trace with a linear or near-linear scan
($\tilde{O}((N+|\Etop|)H)$, no LLM call). Edge counts are $\Theta(N)$ for star, chain,
and tree, and $\Theta(N^2)$ for a dense mesh---why mesh is the costliest topology
(\Cref{app:pilot}). Estimating the metrics to accuracy $\epsilon$ costs
$M=O(\epsilon^{-2}\ln(1/\alpha))$ episodes (\Cref{thm:sample}); a \corb{} run of $R$
rounds costs $\Theta(RMNH)$ invocations; a sweep of $G$ configurations resolving $\NRPs$
gaps of size $\Delta$ costs $\Theta(GNH\Delta^{-2}\ln(G/\alpha))$ (\Cref{cor:budget}).
Because the parameter space is exponential in the number of adversary knobs, \corb{}
explores a \emph{reachable region} rather than the whole space---an honesty constraint we
report per study.
